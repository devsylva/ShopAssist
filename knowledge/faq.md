# Velora — Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**How do I find my order ID?**
Your order ID (format: SA-XXXXX) is in your order confirmation email. You can also find it by logging into your account and going to Order History.

**Can I change or cancel my order?**
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement, provided they have not yet entered processing. After that, the order cannot be changed. Contact support@velora.shop immediately with your order ID if you need to cancel.

**Why hasn't my order shipped yet?**
Most orders are processed and shipped within 1–2 business days. During peak periods (holidays, sales), processing may take up to 3 business days. You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order ships.

**My order shows "Processing" — what does that mean?**
"Processing" means your payment was confirmed and your order is being prepared for shipment. You'll get a shipping notification email shortly.

**I received the wrong item. What do I do?**
We're sorry about that! Contact support@velora.shop within 7 days of delivery with your order ID and a photo of the incorrect item. We'll send the correct item at no charge and arrange a free return of the wrong one.

**My item arrived damaged. What do I do?**
Please email support@velora.shop within 7 days of delivery with your order ID and photos of the damage. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund depending on availability.

## Payments

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Velora accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.

**Is my payment information secure?**
Yes. All payments are processed through Stripe, which uses bank-level encryption. Velora never stores your full card details.

**Why was my payment declined?**
Common reasons include incorrect card details, insufficient funds, or a bank security hold. If your payment is declined, try a different card or contact your bank. If the issue persists, email support@velora.shop.

**When will I be charged?**
Your card is charged at the time of order placement, not at shipment.

## Products

**Are your products available in stores?**
No, Velora is exclusively online at velora.shop.

**Do you restock sold-out items?**
Most popular items are restocked within 4–8 weeks. Sign up for the "Notify Me" alert on any product page to get an email when it's back.

**Do products come with a warranty?**
Velora offers a 90-day manufacturing defect warranty on all products. This covers defects in materials and workmanship, but not normal wear and tear or damage from misuse.
