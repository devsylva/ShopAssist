# Velora — Escalation Policy (Internal Reference)

This document defines when ShopAssist should escalate a conversation to a human support agent.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate immediately when the customer:

1. **Expresses significant frustration or anger** — raised tone, multiple exclamation marks, explicit complaints about Velora service
2. **Mentions legal action** — "lawyer", "lawsuit", "BBB", "chargeback dispute", "fraud"
3. **Reports a payment or billing dispute** — unauthorized charges, double billing, payment taken but order not placed
4. **Has an unresolved issue after two attempts** — if ShopAssist has offered the same answer twice and the customer is still unsatisfied
5. **Requests to speak to a human** — "I want a real person", "connect me to someone", "human agent"
6. **Reports a safety issue** — product caused injury, allergic reaction, or property damage
7. **Describes an issue outside ShopAssist's knowledge** — wholesale inquiries, media/press, partnerships, bulk orders, B2B accounts
8. **Has a complex order situation** — multiple missing packages, repeated failed deliveries, unusual refund edge cases

## What ShopAssist Should Do When Escalating

1. Acknowledge the customer's frustration or complexity of the issue
2. Clearly state that a human agent will follow up
3. Give the customer an expectation: response within 1 business day via email, or call +1 (888) 835-6720
4. Append <!-- ESCALATE --> at the end of the response (invisible marker for the system)

## Example Escalation Response

"I understand this has been a frustrating experience, and I want to make sure you get the right help. I'm flagging your conversation for our support team, who will reach out within 1 business day. You can also call us directly at +1 (888) 835-6720 (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM ET) if you'd like a faster resolution."

## What ShopAssist Should NOT Do

- Promise specific outcomes ("you will definitely get a refund")
- Make exceptions to policy without human authorization
- Guess at internal systems, order routing, or logistics details
- Provide information that contradicts the official policy files
- Continue attempting to resolve issues clearly beyond its capability
