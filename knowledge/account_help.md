# Velora — Account Help

## Creating an Account

Visit velora.shop and click "Sign Up" in the top right corner. You'll need:
- A valid email address
- A password (minimum 8 characters)

You can also check out as a guest without creating an account, but you won't be able to track orders online or access order history without an account.

## Logging In

Go to velora.shop and click "Log In". Enter the email and password associated with your account.

If you're getting an "Invalid credentials" error, make sure you're using the email address from your original sign-up. Check for typos and confirm caps lock is off.

## Forgot Password

1. Click "Log In" on velora.shop
2. Click "Forgot your password?"
3. Enter your email address and click Submit
4. Check your inbox for a password reset link (expires in 24 hours)
5. Check your spam/junk folder if you don't see it within 5 minutes

If you no longer have access to the email address on your account, contact support@velora.shop for manual verification.

## Changing Your Password

While logged in, go to **Account Settings → Security → Change Password**. You'll need your current password to set a new one.

## Updating Your Email Address

Go to **Account Settings → Personal Information → Edit Email**. A verification link will be sent to your new email address before the change is confirmed.

## Updating Your Shipping Address

You can manage saved addresses in **Account Settings → Addresses**. Note: updating your default address here does not change the shipping address on orders already placed.

## Order History

Log in and go to **Account → Order History** to see all past and current orders, tracking info, and invoices.

## Deleting Your Account

To permanently delete your account and associated data, email support@velora.shop with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address on your account. We'll process your request within 5 business days. Note: deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Privacy and Data

Velora collects your email, name, and shipping address to process orders. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. For the full privacy policy, visit velora.shop/privacy.

## Newsletter and Marketing Emails

To unsubscribe from marketing emails, click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any Velora email. You'll still receive transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates) as long as you have an active account.

## Technical Issues with the Website

If you're experiencing problems accessing your account or placing an order:
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser or device
- Disable browser extensions temporarily

If issues persist, contact support@velora.shop with a description of the problem and your browser/device type.
