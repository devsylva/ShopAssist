# Velora — Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Shipping Options (United States)

| Method         | Delivery Time        | Cost                          |
|----------------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Standard       | 5–7 business days    | $5.99 (free on orders ≥ $75)  |
| Express        | 2–3 business days    | $12.99                        |
| Next Day       | 1 business day       | $24.99                        |

*Delivery times are estimated from the date of shipment, not order placement.*

## Free Shipping

Standard shipping is free on all US orders of **$75 or more** (after discounts, before tax). Free shipping is applied automatically at checkout.

## Order Processing Time

- Most orders ship within **1–2 business days** of placement
- During high-volume periods (holidays, sales), processing may take up to **3 business days**
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET may not ship until the following business day
- Weekend and holiday orders are processed the next business day

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with:
- Your tracking number
- A link to track your package in real time

You can also find tracking info in your account under Order History. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after the shipping label is created.

## Shipping Carriers

Velora ships via **UPS**, **USPS**, and **FedEx** depending on your location and the shipping method selected.

## International Shipping

Velora currently ships to **Canada, the UK, and Australia**.

| Destination | Delivery Time        | Cost       |
|-------------|----------------------|------------|
| Canada      | 7–14 business days   | $14.99     |
| UK          | 10–18 business days  | $19.99     |
| Australia   | 14–21 business days  | $24.99     |

*International customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes. These are not included in the shipping fee.*

## Delivery Issues

**Package not delivered but marked delivered:**
Wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark packages early. If it still hasn't arrived, check with neighbors and your building's mail area. Then contact support@velora.shop with your order ID.

**Package lost in transit:**
If tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days, contact us at support@velora.shop. We'll file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or issue a refund.

**Delivery to PO Boxes:**
Standard shipping is available to PO Boxes via USPS. Express and Next Day delivery are not available to PO Boxes.

**Address changes:**
Address changes can be made within 2 hours of order placement. After that, we cannot redirect the package once it's with the carrier.

## Holidays and Delays

Shipping carriers do not operate on US federal holidays. Orders placed on or just before holidays may experience delays. We recommend ordering at least 7 business days before any date-sensitive events.
