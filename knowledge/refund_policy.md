# Velora — Refund & Return Policy

## Return Window

Velora accepts returns within **30 days of the delivery date**. Items returned after 30 days will not be accepted.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, items must be:
- Unused and in their original condition
- In the original packaging (box, wrapping, tags attached)
- Accompanied by proof of purchase (order ID)

**Items that cannot be returned:**
- Final sale or clearance items (marked at checkout)
- Personalized or custom-made items
- Digital downloads or gift cards
- Items marked as non-returnable on the product page
- Opened consumable products (candles that have been burned, opened essential oils)

## How to Start a Return

1. Email support@velora.shop with subject: "Return Request — [Your Order ID]"
2. Include your order ID, item name, reason for return, and photos if applicable
3. Our team will respond within 1 business day with a prepaid return label
4. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any UPS location
5. Once received and inspected (1–3 business days), your refund will be processed

## Refund Processing

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method only**
- Processing time: **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the return
- You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is issued
- Your bank may take an additional 2–5 business days to post the credit

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different item, return the original (if eligible) and place a new order.

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, contact us within **7 days of delivery**. We will cover all return shipping costs and either send a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.

## Cancelled Orders

Orders cancelled within 2 hours of placement receive a full refund within 3–5 business days. If an order is cancelled after shipment, it follows the standard return process.

## Refund Eligibility by Order Status

- **Processing / Shipped / In Transit:** Contact us — we may be able to cancel before delivery
- **Delivered (within 30 days):** Return eligible per the process above
- **Delivered (after 30 days):** Not eligible for return
- **Cancelled:** Refund processed automatically within 5 business days
- **Payment Failed:** No charge was made; no refund needed
