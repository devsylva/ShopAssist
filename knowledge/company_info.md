# Velora — Company Information

## About Velora

Velora is an online retailer specializing in home decor, wellness products, and lifestyle accessories. We design and curate products that help people create calm, beautiful, and functional spaces at home.

Founded in 2019, Velora ships across the United States and to select international destinations. All products are designed with quality and sustainability in mind.
